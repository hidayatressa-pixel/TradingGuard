# TradingGuard V0.9 — Risk Sizing Architecture Lock

## Purpose

V0.9 introduces an authoritative backend Risk Sizing Engine before operational PAPER BUY/SELL is enabled.

The sizing engine answers one narrow question:

> Given account equity, intended entry price, stop-loss price, and an allowed risk budget, what position quantity/notional can be opened without exceeding that risk budget?

It does **not** predict market direction and does **not** grant execution permission by itself.

## Locked separation of concepts

These values are different and MUST NOT be aliased:

- `position_size_pct`: capital allocation used by the existing V0.7 paper engine.
- `risk_budget_pct`: maximum account equity intentionally exposed to loss at the stop.
- `risk_per_trade_pct`: calculated account-equity loss percentage at the proposed stop.
- `stop_distance_pct`: distance between entry and stop relative to entry.

The prohibited mapping remains:

`position_size_pct -> risk_per_trade_pct`

Allocation is not risk.

## Authoritative inputs

A valid prospective long position requires all of:

- finite positive account equity;
- finite positive entry price;
- finite positive stop-loss price;
- stop-loss strictly below entry for a long position;
- finite `risk_budget_pct` greater than zero;
- optional maximum allocation cap, finite and in `(0, 100]` when supplied.

Missing, invalid, zero, negative, NaN, or infinite required values fail closed. No missing risk fact is converted to zero.

## Long-position sizing math

For a long entry:

- `risk_budget_amount = equity * risk_budget_pct / 100`
- `loss_per_unit_at_stop = entry_price - stop_loss_price`
- `quantity_by_risk = risk_budget_amount / loss_per_unit_at_stop`
- `notional_by_risk = quantity_by_risk * entry_price`

If an allocation cap is supplied:

- `max_allocation_amount = equity * max_allocation_pct / 100`
- final notional is capped at `min(notional_by_risk, max_allocation_amount)`;
- final quantity is final notional divided by entry price.

The resulting authoritative prospective risk is then recalculated from the final quantity:

- `risk_amount = final_quantity * loss_per_unit_at_stop`
- `risk_per_trade_pct = risk_amount / equity * 100`

The engine must not claim the requested risk budget was fully used when an allocation cap reduces the position.

## Fees and slippage

Initial V0.9 sizing must explicitly state whether fees/slippage are included in risk-at-stop. They must never be silently ignored while claiming an all-in loss figure.

For the first implementation checkpoint, sizing may expose price-risk separately from estimated costs. Operational PAPER entry remains disabled until the treatment of costs is covered by tests and documented.

## RiskContext bridge

Risk Sizing may provide authoritative `risk_per_trade_pct` for a proposed entry only after valid sizing inputs exist.

It does not fabricate the other V0.5 `RiskContext` facts:

- `daily_loss_pct`
- `total_exposure_pct`
- `open_positions`
- `current_drawdown_pct`
- `trading_enabled`

Those facts require their own authoritative backend account/risk-state sources. Until the complete RiskContext exists, Risk Guard remains unavailable/fail-closed for prospective entry.

## Execution authority

The future entry pipeline is:

`Market -> Indicators -> Strategy -> Risk Sizing -> complete RiskContext -> Risk Guard -> PAPER execution`

Only `RiskDecision.ALLOW` may authorize a new PAPER entry.

`WARNING`, `BLOCK`, incomplete context, sizing failure, disconnected market data, or invalid/nonfinite values do not authorize entry.

Strategy assessment alone never authorizes execution.

## Exit safety

Risk-context unavailability for a new entry must not trap an already-open position. Exit/reduce-risk behavior will be governed separately from opening-risk authorization.

## Frontend boundary

React may collect/display sizing inputs and backend results. React must not calculate authoritative quantity, risk-per-trade, stop risk, RiskContext, or execution permission.

## Out of scope for this checkpoint

- live broker execution;
- exchange API keys;
- real-money custody;
- automatic BUY/SELL;
- short-position sizing;
- leverage/margin;
- trailing stops;
- frontend risk calculations;
- silently converting paper allocation into risk.

## Required implementation gates

Before V0.9 sizing can feed PAPER entry:

1. backend sizing models/service are deterministic and finite-safe;
2. tests cover normal sizing, allocation cap, invalid stop, zero/negative/nonfinite values;
3. `position_size_pct` is never interpreted as `risk_per_trade_pct`;
4. price-risk vs fees/slippage treatment is explicit;
5. complete RiskContext source is separately implemented and tested;
6. existing V0.7 paper sequencing/regression tests remain green;
7. frontend remains presentation/input only;
8. README is updated truthfully;
9. Technical PASS precedes any operational PAPER BUY/SELL activation.
