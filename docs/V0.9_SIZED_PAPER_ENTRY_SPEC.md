# TradingGuard V0.9 — Sized Paper Entry Specification

## Purpose

Close the quantity mismatch between Risk Sizing and Paper Trading before Paper BUY is activated.

## Locked rules

1. A prospective Paper entry must execute the exact authoritative `final_quantity` produced by Risk Sizing for that proposal. Paper Trading must not recalculate quantity from `position_size_pct` for a sized entry.
2. The sizing proposal is bound to its stop-loss. `final_quantity` and `stop_loss_price` travel together into `PendingEntry` and the resulting `PaperPosition`.
3. A sized entry may be scheduled only when the prospective Risk Gate has established `ALLOW`. WARNING, BLOCK, unavailable context, sizing failure, or invalid data must not schedule entry.
4. `position_size_pct` remains legacy V0.7 allocation behavior for the existing legacy `process_candle` path only. It is not risk-per-trade and must not be used by the new sized-entry path.
5. Before scheduling, the sizing equity must match the authoritative Paper account realized equity within a small floating-point tolerance. Stale or foreign sizing must fail closed.
6. Before execution, quantity must be finite and positive, stop must be finite and positive, and stop must remain below the effective long entry price.
7. Execution still applies the existing adverse entry slippage and transaction fee. If the sized quantity plus fee exceeds available cash, execution fails closed; quantity must not be silently reduced because that would break the audited sizing proposal.
8. Because V0.9 Risk Sizing currently excludes fees/slippage/gap risk from `risk_per_trade_pct`, this path is Paper-only and must disclose that planned price risk is not guaranteed maximum loss.
9. Existing V0.7 next-candle-open semantics remain: authorization at event T schedules entry for event T+1 OPEN.
10. Existing stop-loss semantics remain authoritative after entry.
11. No live broker execution, credentials, leverage, shorting, or real-money activation is introduced.

## Required model additions

`PendingEntry` must be able to carry an optional authoritative `quantity`. Legacy entries keep `quantity=None`; sized entries require a positive finite quantity.

## Required tests

- sized entry persists exact Risk Sizing `final_quantity` into the open position;
- sized entry does not fall back to `position_size_pct`;
- allocation cap affects Risk Sizing quantity once, not again in Paper;
- stale sizing equity fails closed;
- WARNING/BLOCK/unavailable context cannot schedule sized entry;
- invalid/nonfinite quantity fails closed;
- insufficient cash fails closed rather than silently resizing;
- next-open timing and stop propagation remain unchanged;
- legacy V0.7 regression suite remains green.

## Activation gate

This stage may add an internal sized-entry adapter and tests. The public/UI Paper BUY control remains disabled until the adapter, API orchestration, and full regression suite pass.
